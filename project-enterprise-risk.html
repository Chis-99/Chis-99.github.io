The Challenge

IslandLink Business Services Ltd. is a fictional Jamaican mid-sized company with approximately 150 employees operating from Kingston and Montego Bay.

The organization uses a hybrid work model and relies heavily on Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID, Windows endpoints, cloud-based accounting, CRM and HR systems, SharePoint, OneDrive, VPN access, corporate Wi-Fi, legacy Active Directory, third-party IT providers and cloud services.

Security controls existed across the environment, but rapid growth and incremental technology adoption resulted in inconsistent governance, fragmented monitoring, legacy infrastructure and several high-impact control gaps.

Enterprise Risk Assessment

The threat scenarios were consolidated into 22 formal enterprise cybersecurity risks.

Each risk includes a business risk statement, threat source, vulnerability, existing controls, accountable owner, treatment decision and evidence requirements.

Risk Treatment Program

Eighteen remediation actions were organized into risk-reduction workstreams with accountable owners, due dates, dependencies, evidence requirements and measurable success criteria.

The modeled program represents approximately 252 person-days of implementation effort.

Budget requirements were expressed as qualitative tiers because vendor pricing and real implementation costs would vary.

30 / 60 / 90-Day Remediation Roadmap

0–30 Days: Universal MFA, payment-verification controls, backup protection, perimeter hardening and cyber-risk governance.

31–60 Days: Privileged-access governance, financial-integrity controls, restoration testing, firewall/VPN hardening and formal risk appetite.

61–90 Days: Endpoint patch compliance, secure configurations, laptop encryption, asset reconciliation and vulnerability review.

90+ Days: Centralized monitoring, incident response, vendor risk management, DLP, security awareness, formal policies, legacy-system migration and infrastructure resilience.

Assessment Outcome

Elevated Current Cyber Risk — Priority Remediation Required

The organization demonstrates several foundational cybersecurity capabilities, particularly around asset identification and risk assessment.

However, material weaknesses remain in identity security, privileged access, ransomware resilience, legacy infrastructure, perimeter protection, monitoring, supplier governance and recovery.

Projected residual risk should only be formally accepted after remediation controls are implemented, tested and supported by operating evidence.
