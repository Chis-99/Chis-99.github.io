How I Approach Cybersecurity Risk

1. Understand the Business

Identify important services, systems, information, stakeholders and dependencies before assessing technical risk.

2. Assess the Risk

Connect assets, threats, vulnerabilities and business impact to create traceable and defensible risk decisions.

3. Map the Controls

Evaluate current safeguards against relevant frameworks, evidence requirements and the organization's target state.

4. Drive Remediation

Translate findings into prioritized actions with owners, milestones, success criteria and measurable evidence.
